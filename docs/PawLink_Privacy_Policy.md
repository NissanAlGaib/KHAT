# PawLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 21, 2026
Last updated: April 21, 2026

PawLink ("we", "us", or "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the PawLink mobile application and related services.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use PawLink.

## 1) Who We Are

PawLink is a pet breeding matchmaking platform that connects breeders and shooters and supports related features such as profiles, matching, chat, contracts, payment records, and safety reporting.

App package name: com.khat.pawlink

## 2) Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

### A. Account and Profile Information
- Name
- Email address
- Contact number
- Account credentials and authentication tokens
- User role data (for example breeder or shooter)

### B. Pet and Verification Information
- Pet profile details (such as species, breed, age, health and vaccination records)
- User-submitted verification documents and metadata
- Photos and files uploaded by you

### C. Camera and Photo Access
PawLink may access your camera and media library when you choose to:
- Take a photo in-app
- Upload a photo or document
- Use image-based features (for example breed identification)

We only access camera or media with your permission. You can disable camera or media permissions in your device settings.

### D. Location Information
PawLink may collect approximate or precise location data when enabled by you to:
- Show nearby users or pets
- Improve location-based matching and discovery

You can disable location permissions in your device settings, but some features may not work as intended.

### E. Payment and Transaction Information
- Transaction records (amount, currency, status, timestamps, references)
- Subscription and billing metadata

Payment card or wallet credentials are handled by payment providers and are not fully stored by PawLink servers.

### F. Device and Usage Information
- App activity logs
- Crash and diagnostics data
- Device identifiers and technical metadata (such as OS version and app version)

### G. Communication and Support Data
- Messages you send in app features
- Reports, complaints, and support requests
- Safety and moderation records

## 3) How We Use Information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage accounts
- Provide app features and user support
- Facilitate matching, chat, contracts, and related workflows
- Process and record transactions and subscriptions
- Verify user identities and documents
- Improve platform safety, prevent fraud, and enforce policies
- Monitor performance, troubleshoot errors, and improve user experience
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4) How We Share Information

We may share information:
- With other users as required by app functionality (for example profile and pet details shown in matching workflows)
- With service providers that help us operate the platform (hosting, storage, analytics, notifications, payments)
- With payment processors for transaction handling
- With legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security
- During business transfers such as merger, acquisition, or asset sale

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## 5) Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for:
- Service delivery
- Security and fraud prevention
- Legal, tax, and accounting obligations
- Dispute resolution and policy enforcement

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it where feasible.

## 6) Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7) Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:
- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your account and personal data
- Withdraw consent for optional permissions such as camera or location

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 11.

## 8) Children

PawLink is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## 9) International Processing

Your data may be processed and stored in countries outside your own. We use reasonable safeguards for cross-border data handling where required.
